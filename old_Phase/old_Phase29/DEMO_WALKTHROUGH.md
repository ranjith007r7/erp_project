### 2. CRM → the pipeline
- Show the **3 leads** — Anjali (still a raw lead), Rohan and Fatima (converted).

### 4. Inventory → the low-stock story, live

### 5. Procurement → the other half of the stock story

### 6. Finance → where the money actually is

### 7. HR & Payroll → the other side of the business

### 9. Settings → the two "customizable base" payoffs

This is the actual security model, demonstrable live, not just described.

## If asked "what happens if I break something on purpose"

Two good live moments, if the room wants to see resilience, not just happy paths:

- **Try to over-sell a low-stock item.** Attempt a quotation for more Visitor Chairs than exist — the system rejects it and stock stays untouched. Nothing partially breaks.
- **Log in as the Sales Viewer and try to create something.** A clean, clear permission error — not a crash, not a silent failure.

## What this demo does *not* show (say this proactively, don't wait to be asked)

- **Projects & Tasks** is deliberately unseeded — an empty module ready to configure, a good moment to say "every module here starts empty and gets shaped to a client's actual workflow, this one just hasn't been touched yet for this demo."
- Email verification and password reset exist and work, but no real email provider is connected yet (said plainly, not hidden) — reset links currently land in server logs, not inboxes.
- RBAC enforcement covers every route in every module — the roadmap's original "utmost features" ask — but the two-org isolation and privilege-escalation protections are things worth mentioning as *already tested*, not things to click through live (they're best proven with automated tests, not a live demo).
